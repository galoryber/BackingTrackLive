v0.1.9 shipped the align view and no way to reach it. This is the release that
makes it usable.

## Select a track

The track table was all widgets and no row, so there was nothing to click that
meant "this one" — and the **align** button, which acts on the selected track,
was permanently greyed out. Clicking a row now selects it, and adding a stem
selects it, since the one you just added is the one you want to work on.

## Move around inside a song

Checking a stem meant listening from the top. For a part that enters thirty
seconds in, that is thirty seconds per attempt — and the question that matters
most about alignment, *is it still in time at the last chorus*, lives at the
end of a four-minute song.

Play and stop are now a transport: a position scrub, a readout against the
song's length, and buttons for **start** and the **last 20s**. In both the song
editor and the align view.

## How to check a stem now

1. Edit the song, click the stem's row, **align →**
2. **snap start to beat 1**, or drag the waveform
3. **play**, then **last 20s**

If it is still tight at the end, lead-in silence was the whole problem and you
are done. If it has drifted, the tempo is wrong rather than the offset, and no
nudge fixes that — that is a tempo map, which the file format supports and the
editor does not yet expose.

## Notes

The coverage floor caught `length_bars` being written by nothing that read it
back. Fixing that exposed a larger hole: the save/load comparison every
round-trip test runs through never looked at `length_bars`, so it would have
passed over any new song field, silently. Both directions are now verified by
mutation.

## Still not here

- **Tempo maps and track reordering** in the editor.
- **ASIO**, and **MIDI**.
