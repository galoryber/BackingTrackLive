End a song where you actually stop playing it, and write yourself a note.

## Ending a song on a bar

**"ends at bar"** in the song editor now ends the song there, whether or not
the stems have finished. It used only to extend a song past its stems; shorter
than the stems it was ignored, so the only way to play a song to bar 114 and
segue into the next one was to edit the audio file.

A song that ends before its stems do **fades out** over the last second,
because cutting a sustaining stem off mid-note is a click — and on a segue that
click lands in the gap between two songs, where everyone hears it. A song that
ends where its stems end is left alone: there is nothing to cut, and fading it
would alter audio that had already finished.

## A cue line

A free-text note per song, shown under the title while it plays and nowhere
else. *"drums in at 24"*, *"vocals B34"* — whatever is worth reading mid-song.

## Tempo on the align screen

Beside the nudge, and live. A guessed BPM is something you fix by watching the
grid against the waveform, which is that screen — and the change takes effect
while the song plays, so you can hear the click move onto the beat.

## Smaller

**Saving a song stops the audition.** Leaving it playing while the screen went
back to the set list left you hunting for where the sound was coming from.
