A segue you cannot miss, and spoken count-in cues without the arithmetic.

## SEGUE

Now a filled orange badge rather than yellow text. At the back of a stage
under lights a block of colour reads and a word does not — and this is the one
thing on the screen that changes what the band does when the song ends, so it
should not look like the click, which sharing the click's yellow amounted to.

The set list marks a segue too, where the set actually gets planned.

## Spoken count-in cues

Record a lead-in — *"starts on beat three with the guitar riff, here we go"* —
in whatever you already use, add it as a stem, route it to **`inear`**, open
the align view and press **start on the count-in**. It plays over the click,
the audience hears nothing, and it stops when the song begins.

This already worked: the count-in is not a special region to the engine, the
playhead is simply negative there, and a track's offset is subtracted from it.
What was missing was not having to work out that two bars of 4/4 at 148 BPM is
−3243 ms.

**`btcheck` no longer calls it a mistake.** It used to warn whenever a stem
landed entirely before the song started, which is exactly what a cue does. It
now warns only when a stem lands before even the count-in, where nothing will
hear it.

Recording inside the program is still not built, and is still the right thing
to leave until last — see [docs/roadmap.md](../roadmap.md). A phone covers
three songs; if the band ends up wanting a cue on every song, that is the
evidence that changes the answer.
