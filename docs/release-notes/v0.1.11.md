A segue now counts in when the next song asks for one.

## The join between two songs

Running one song into the next went straight to bar 1, always. The reasoning
was that counting in is not what "plays straight into the next one" means —
which is true of two songs in the same tempo, and wrong the moment the tempo
changes. Without those bars the click simply changes speed mid-flow, with
nothing to carry the band across.

It was never the player's decision. `count_in_bars` is already a per-song
setting: **zero means straight in**, which is what two musically continuous
songs want, and **anything else means give us the new tempo first**. The
segue now honours whichever the incoming song asks for, and the count-in
ticks at the new song's tempo.

## Align view

**"Snap start to beat 1" is gone.** It assumed a stem's first audible sound is
its downbeat — true of a full backing track, false of any stem that enters
partway through a song. On a synth part arriving thirty seconds in it moved
the stem thirty seconds early, with nothing to undo it. That was a wrong idea
rather than a bad threshold, so it is removed rather than tuned. Nudging
against the waveform does the job: the case it was built for took one drag.

Where a stem's audio begins is still reported, as a time rather than as an
error against beat 1.

**The playhead is drawn whether or not the song is playing**, and the view
follows a seek as well as playback — dragging the scrub while stopped is how
you look at the end of a song without listening to it, and it previously
showed nothing.

**The selected row** in the track table is highlighted at the height of the
row rather than of a line of text.
